Data Deletion Instructions

Personal Assistant is a private, single-user app. All data it holds belongs to its owner and is stored only on the owner's own computer.

To delete your data:

  1. Remove the app's access in the provider's settings (Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove the app; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  2. Delete the app's local data folder on the owner's computer, which removes every stored message, token and summary.

Removing access stops all further collection immediately. Contact the developer via the email shown on the app's sign-in screens with any questions.
